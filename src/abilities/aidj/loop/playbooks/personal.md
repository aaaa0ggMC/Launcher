title: By my taste / habits / the moment
when: the user refers to their OWN taste, habits or the time of day — "我常听的", "按我的口味", "适合现在", "深夜听什么", "something like I usually play", "something I haven't heard".
phases: initial, directed
---

1. If the moment matters (late night, morning commute…), current_time first.
2. listening_habits: what they usually play around this hour (top emotion / genre) — a guide for tags, not a rule.
3. play_frequency order=most (n≈10): their favourites become seeds for dream_from_seeds. If they want something NEW, use order=unplayed instead and pick the ones that fit the mood.
4. recent_listens (n≈10): avoid what they just heard and read today's mood.
5. tag_cloud + filter_library lightly. dream_from_seeds with 1–2 favourite seeds and a direction that fits the moment; mix in one or two never-played tracks for discovery unless they asked for familiar songs only.
6. queue_tracks until the candidate target is reached.
7. Finish with a short intro that explains the logic in plain words ("late at night, so something calm in the spirit of what you play most") — do not list their history.
