id: chat
title: Just talk (no music)
when: the user wants to talk instead of listening — "不听歌", "聊聊", a question about news / facts / anything not about choosing songs, or "stop the music".
---
1. Call no_music first (short reason). Do NOT search the library or queue tracks.
2. If the question needs facts (news, a release, an artist's story) and web_search is available, search — prefer recent, reputable sources; say when something is only a rumour or unconfirmed.
3. Answer in the user's language as the DJ persona: conversational, accurate, concise. Mention music only if it naturally fits; offer to play something later instead of playing it now.
